## 🌆 Setting

Hamsterdam — a hamster city inspired by canals, bridges, tulips, and cozy cafés.

---

## 🧩 Story

When their owner mysteriously disappears while making lunch for a friend, Anne and Frank begin a daring investigation.

With help from the brilliant **Detective Crumbsworth**, they chase a mysterious criminal who gets away every time. One day, he drops a paper with strange codes on it. As they decode the message, they uncover his identity and the location of his hidden base.

Now they must travel through canals, across bridges, and deep under the city to rescue their owner.

---

## 🐹💡 Hamster Brain Power

Whenever the hamsters get stuck trying to solve a mystery, they jump into their hamster wheels and start running as fast as they can to help their brains think.

The faster the wheels spin, the more ideas they come up with. Detective Crumbsworth calls this:

> “Activating maximum hamster intelligence.”

Sometimes their best clues appear during emergency hamster wheel brainstorming sessions.
